# Support requests past their SLA

**Alert:** `[env] Backlog: support overdue`. At least one help or feedback request has
gone past its first-answer or resolution target for 15 minutes.

1. Open back office → Support inbox → *Past their SLA*.
2. **Urgent** requests first (safety, money). Answer, or at least acknowledge with a
   canned reply and say when you'll be back. A safety request means following
   [the safety incident runbook](safety-incident.md) now.
3. Assign each to someone on shift.
4. If the backlog is from volume (more than one person can clear in an hour), call the
   Support lead to bring in a second person, and post in #ops.
5. If requests are about the same problem (a bug, an outage), open an incident, pin a
   canned reply for it, and answer them all with it.

The alert clears once nothing is past its SLA. Requests don't stop counting when it's
outside staffed hours (8am to 10pm IST), so a quiet night can still leave a backlog in
the morning.
