# First-gig call script

**What it's for:** helping a host who has signed up but hasn't posted a gig (the tracker
says *Next step: First gig drafted* for 3 days or more) write a good first gig, on a
20-minute call. **The host types and sends it themselves.** We never sign in as them.

**Before the call:**
- Open their row in the onboarding tracker, then their timeline (click the phone
  number): property, photos, verification, any support requests.
- Look at two live gigs of the same kind for ideas.
- Book a slot by WhatsApp: "Hi <name>, it's <you> from Barterly. Could we take 20
  minutes to write your first gig together? Today 4pm or tomorrow 11am?"

## 1. Open (2 minutes)

> "Thanks for making time. We'll write your first gig together. You'll type it in your
> console while I help with the wording, and you send it when you're happy. Do you have
> the console open?"

If they're stuck before the gig (property or verification), help with that first and
book the gig call for another day.

## 2. The need (5 minutes)

Ask, and note their words, which make the best gig text:

1. "What's the one thing you most need help with in the next two months?"
   (Murals, social media, front desk, running treks, cooking…)
2. "What would a normal day look like for them? Morning, afternoon, evening?"
3. "How many hours a day, and how many days a week?" Steer to **4 to 5 hours, 5 to 6
   days** for volunteers. More than 6 hours a day draws far fewer applicants.
4. "When would they start, and for how long?" Steer to a start **at least 3 weeks
   away** and **2 to 6 weeks** long.

## 3. The offer (4 minutes)

5. "Where will they sleep? Dorm bed or a private room?"
6. "Which meals are included?"
7. "Is there a stipend?" If yes: explain escrow in one line ("You pay it into Barterly,
   we hold it, and they get it after the stay starts and you approve the work"), and
   the beta limit of ₹20,000.
8. For creators: "What should they post? How many reels or posts, and by when?"

## 4. Write it (6 minutes)

Have them open **Gigs → Post a gig** and go step by step.

- **Title:** role, place and the hook, under 60 characters. E.g. "Mural artist for our
  riverside café in Manali".
- **Description:** three short paragraphs:
  1. Who you are and the place (2 lines).
  2. What they'll do, and a normal day (from question 2).
  3. What they get: stay, meals, stipend, and one thing that makes it special.
- **Who can apply:** ask for ID verification; skills and languages only if they truly
  matter. Every extra requirement means fewer applicants.

Read it back to them out loud. Fix anything that sounds like a job ad
("candidate must…"): we want "you'll…".

## 5. Send and close (3 minutes)

> "Looks great. Tap **Submit for review**. We check it within a day, and you'll get a
> notification when it's live. Applications usually start within a couple of days."

- Tell them where applicants appear (**Applicants**) and that they can chat before
  offering.
- If anything worried you (safety, unrealistic hours, asking for deposits from talent),
  don't argue on the call. Tell the Trust lead and note it in the gig review.

## After the call

- Note in the wave sheet: date, who called, gig sent (yes/no).
- In the back office gig queue, review their gig the same day if you can.
- Follow up in 3 days if the gig has no applicants: check the title and dates together.
