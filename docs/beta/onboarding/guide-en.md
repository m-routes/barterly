# Welcome to Barterly: getting started as a host

Thank you for joining the Barterly beta. Barterly connects your property with artists,
creators, volunteers and trip leaders who trade their skills for a stay. During the
beta you're one of a small group of hosts, so your feedback shapes what we build.

You'll need your **invite code** (it looks like `ABCD-2345`) and about **30 minutes**
for the first time.

## 1. Sign up (5 minutes)

1. Open **barterly.in** on your laptop or phone browser and tap **Sign in**.
2. Enter your mobile number, then the 6-digit code we text you.
3. Choose **I run a place**, enter your **invite code**, and accept the terms.

## 2. Tell us about your business (5 minutes)

Your business name, type (hostel, homestay, café, camp, tour…), city and contact.
Talent see your business name on your gigs.

## 3. Add your property (10 minutes)

- The address, then **pin it on the map**. Talent see the area, never the exact
  address, until they're booked.
- At least **3 photos**: the common area, where talent will sleep, and the outside.
- Amenities: Wi-Fi, kitchen, lockers and so on.

## 4. Get verified (5 minutes, then up to 2 days)

Under **Verification**, verify your ID and your property. Verified hosts get a badge on
every gig, and talent apply more to verified places. We check within 2 days.

## 5. Post your first gig (10 minutes)

Under **Gigs → Post a gig**, five short steps:

1. **What you need:** pick a role (volunteer, content creator, trip leader or
   specialist), a clear title and what the work is.
2. **When:** dates, hours per day, days per week.
3. **What you offer:** the stay, meals, and a stipend if you pay one.
4. **Who can apply:** skills, languages, and the verification you want.
5. **Check and send.**

We review every gig within a day before it goes live. During the beta you can have
**2 gigs live at a time**, and a stipend can be at most **₹20,000** per booking.

**A good first gig:** one clear role, dates at least 3 weeks away, 4 to 5 hours a day,
and what a day looks like. If you'd like, we'll write it with you on a call: reply to
our message.

## 6. Choose your talent

Applications arrive in **Applicants**. Shortlist, chat, and send an **offer**. When they
accept, you both sign the agreement with a code on your phone. If you pay a stipend,
you fund it into Barterly's escrow. It's held safely and released to them after the
stay starts and you approve their work.

## 7. On arrival day

Open **Arrivals**, tap **Scan a pass** and scan the pass on their phone. It works without
signal too.

## Help

- In the console: **Help** → *Write to us*. Urgent safety or money problems are answered
  within 2 hours, 8am to 10pm.
- **In an emergency, call 112.**

Thank you for being one of the first. Tell us everything that's confusing: Help →
*Feedback or an idea*.
