# Property onboarding checklist

One row per property, in the order hosts go through it. Steps 2 to 8 are tracked
automatically in back office → **Beta onboarding tracker**; the rest are ops' own.

## Before the invite

1. **Picked for the wave.** It's in the wave's region, takes guests now, and the owner or
   manager agreed on the intro call to try Barterly.
2. **Code made**, labelled with the property's name and town (back office → *Make
   invite codes*, cohort `wave-1`).
3. **Invite sent** by WhatsApp from the ops phone: the code, the link to the web console,
   and the guide ([English](guide-en.md) / [हिंदी](guide-hi.md)). Note the date in the
   wave sheet.

## In the product (tracked)

| # | Step | Tracker column | Nudge if stuck after |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4 | Signed up with the code | Signed up with their code | 3 days after the invite |
| 5 | Business profile: business name, type, city, contact | Business profile filled in | 2 days |
| 6 | Property added, pinned on the map, at least 3 photos | Property added | 2 days |
| 7 | ID and property verification sent, and approved | ID or property verified | 2 days after sending (our queue: 48 h) |
| 8 | First gig drafted | First gig drafted | 3 days: book the first-gig call |
| 9 | First gig sent for review, approved, and live | First gig live | 2 days in review: check the gig queue |
| 10 | First talent hired (offer accepted, both signed) | First talent hired | 14 days live: review the gig with them |

## After the first hire

11. **Check-in day:** call or message the host the morning the first talent arrives. Do
    they know how to scan the pass on the Arrivals screen?
12. **Stay check:** 3 days in, a message to the host and to the talent: anything wrong?
13. **After the stay:** remind both to review. Ask the host for feedback in the app
    (Help → Feedback).
14. **Done:** mark the property *onboarded* in the wave sheet.

## Rules of thumb

- Never fill in the host's forms for them. Sit with them on a call while they do it.
  The exception is the gig text, which we may draft with them
  ([call script](first-gig-call-script.md)).
- Never ask for their password or sign-in code.
- Anything about safety or money that comes up on a call goes in a support request
  for them (so there's a record) and to the Trust lead the same day.
