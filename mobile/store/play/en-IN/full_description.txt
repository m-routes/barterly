Travel India by trading what you're good at.

Barterly connects artists, photographers, creators, musicians, yoga teachers, chefs and other skilled travellers with hostels, homestays and cafés that need their work. You give a few hours a day; the host gives you a bed, meals and sometimes a stipend.

FIND A GIG
• Browse gigs on a map or a list, filtered by skill, dates, city and what's offered.
• See exactly what a host expects: hours, the work, the stay and any stipend.
• Save a search and get told when something new matches.

APPLY IN MINUTES
• Show your work with photos, short videos, PDFs or links.
• Applications you write offline are sent as soon as you're back online.
• Chat with the host, agree the terms and sign the agreement in the app.

TRAVEL WITH CONFIDENCE
• Every gig is reviewed by our team before it goes live, and you can verify your ID.
• Your check-in pass works offline at the property.
• Reviews are double-blind: both sides review before either can see the other's.

GET PAID
• Stipends are held in escrow and released when your stay is confirmed.
• Link a UPI ID or bank account and track every payout and TDS deduction.

Barterly is for adults (18+). It never sells your data or shows ads.
