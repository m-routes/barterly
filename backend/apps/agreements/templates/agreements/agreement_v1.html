5. Content and usage rights

5.1 Ownership. The talent keeps the copyright in everything they create.

5.2 Licence. The talent grants the host a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to use the deliverables on {{ usage.scope_label|lower }}, for {{ usage.duration_months }} month{{ usage.duration_months|pluralize }} from the end of the stay. Any other use needs the talent's written consent.

6. Safety and conduct

6.1 Both parties treat each other with respect. Harassment, discrimination, threats and violence are not allowed and may end this agreement at once.

6.2 The host provides safe accommodation that matches its description, a lockable place for the talent's belongings, drinking water, and the safety equipment and instructions any task needs. The talent is not asked to do work that is dangerous or that needs a licence they do not have.

6.3 The talent follows reasonable house rules shared in advance, and the law.

6.4 Either party can report a safety concern to Barterly at any time. In an emergency, call 112 first.

6.5 The talent may leave at any time if they feel unsafe. Barterly will review what happened before any cancellation counts against either party.

7. Cancellation

7.1 Policy: {{ cancellation_label }}. Either party may cancel without it counting against them up to {{ notice_days }} day{{ notice_days|pluralize }} before the arrival date, by cancelling on Barterly.

7.2 A later cancellation, or not turning up, is recorded on Barterly and may affect the cancelling party's standing.{% if stipend %} Any stipend already paid for days not worked is refunded under Barterly's payment terms.{% endif %}

7.3 Safety reasons under section 6, illness with evidence, and events outside either party's control are not counted against the cancelling party.

8. Changes and the whole agreement

8.1 Changes to these terms are made on Barterly as a new version of this agreement. A new version takes effect only once both parties sign it; until then this version stays in force.

8.2 This agreement, with the gig as described on Barterly and Barterly's terms of use, is the whole agreement between the parties about the stay.
